The Ayni Orchestrator Slice 2 control plane and scripted executor are installed.

Health endpoint:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/control-plane 8080:{{ .Values.controlPlane.httpPort }}

Trusted private-v1 diagnostic endpoint (pod-local only):
  POD=$(kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pod -l app.kubernetes.io/name=ayni-orchestrator-control-plane -o jsonpath='{.items[0].metadata.name}')
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward pod/$POD 50051:{{ .Values.controlPlane.trustedGrpcPort }}

The chart never creates database or broker credentials. It expects four distinct
immutable Secrets: {{ .Values.workflowPostgres.migratorSecretName | quote }},
{{ .Values.workflowPostgres.runtimeSecretName | quote }},
{{ .Values.workflowJetstream.applicationSecretName | quote }}, and
{{ .Values.workflowJetstream.executorSecretName | quote }}. The runtime database
URL must authenticate as {{ .Values.workflowPostgres.runtimeRoleName | quote }}.
The executor PVC is retained after uninstall and must not be deleted while a
command/report/decision handoff is unresolved.
